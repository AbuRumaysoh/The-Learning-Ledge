# The Learning Ledge 🎓

> **Unmasking Ignorance with Knowledge.**

The Learning Ledge is an EdTech platform designed to support secondary school students in their academic journey through accessible learning resources, exam preparation, CBT practice, and student-focused productivity tools.

This project was developed as our capstone project during the ActivateX Academy programme.

---

## 📌 Project Overview

The Learning Ledge was created with the goal of providing students with a simple and engaging digital learning environment where they can access academic resources and prepare more effectively for examinations.

The landing page serves as the main entry point to the platform, introducing visitors to the brand, its purpose, and the learning opportunities available to students.

---

## 🎯 Target Audience

The platform is primarily designed for:

- Secondary school students
- WAEC candidates
- NECO candidates
- JAMB/UTME candidates
- Students looking for accessible learning and exam-preparation resources

---

## ✨ Key Highlights

- Clean and student-focused landing page
- Structured presentation of the platform's offerings
- Responsive layout across different screen sizes
- Clear navigation and user-focused sections
- Educational and exam-preparation focused content
- Modern and accessible visual presentation
