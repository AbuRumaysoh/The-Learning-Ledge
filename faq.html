Frequently asked questions.

Short answers to the questions a new student is most likely to ask.

What is The Learning Ledge?

It is an educational platform concept for structured learning, practice and progress tracking.

Can I use it on my phone?

Yes. The public navigation and student dashboard are responsive for smaller screens.

Where do I manage my profile?

Sign in and open Settings from the student dashboard.

Does signing out delete my profile?

Signing out clears the demo session on the current device. It does not remove the saved profile data unless you clear it from the browser.
